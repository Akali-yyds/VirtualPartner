# Open spatial motion

Use `spatialPose` for arbitrary spatial gestures. Do not choose a canned gesture or guess local arm Euler angles when a hand target expresses the intent. Keep legacy bonePose available for explicit joint control, in separate stages.

Action fields: type="spatialPose", sync="prepare" or "speech", tracks=[...], completion={mode:"idle"|"hold"|"timed"|"restore",seconds:number}. Default completion is idle. `timed` counts AFTER arriving; `hold` persists across replies. Only use restore when asked to resume the prior hold. A normal replacement discards the old hold on the affected group. To release poses explicitly, emit {"type":"poseReset","target":"all"}. target may also name one control group. Reset suppresses remaining actions on the reset groups in this response; unrelated groups and speech continue. Debug pins cannot be overridden.

Tracks have group="leftArm"|"rightArm"|"support"|"torso"|"head", anchor="body"|"head"|"chest"|"shoulder", keys=[...]. Each key has increasing time in seconds (0 <= time <= 30), position:{x,y,z}, optional palm:{x,y,z}, fingers:{x,y,z}, hint:{x,y,z}, stop:boolean. Use 1..32 keys per track. Time is relative to that action, not global. Intermediate keys maintain continuous position velocity unless stop=true. The last key settles. If the first key has time=0, runtime adds a 0.35-second entry transition to ALL keys, preserving their relative timing; it never teleports. A track may override action completion with its own completion object.

Coordinates: character right +X, up +Y, forward +Z. Distances are normalized by head-to-foot height. body origin is the character root at action start, frozen along with orientation. Arms may instead use a moving head/chest/shoulder anchor; positions and hint are offsets from it. Palm/fingers are directions in the frozen character frame and must be provided together, nonparallel. Left/right mean the CHARACTER's side. No manual side mirroring for spatial coordinates. Read the measured reach below: the Euclidean distance from the shoulder must be below the stated total arm reach, preferably with 10% margin. Targets must remain within arm reach and clear of the torso. There is no finger articulation or exact skin contact.

support position is a pelvis OFFSET from its starting pose (not a hand-style absolute position), maximum magnitude 0.2 heights. Both feet stay planted; use for shallow crouches and gentle weight shifts. No foot lifting, stepping, jumping, deep squats, chairs or dynamic balancing. Sequence locomotion before/after spatial actions; never combine with facing, locomotion, animation or bonePose in one stage.

torso/head keys use bones:[{bone:"Spine"|"Chest" or "Neck"|"Head",side:"None",rotation:{x,y,z}}] with existing local base-relative rotation semantics. support may additionally rotate Pelvis. Do not put direct arm rotations in an IK track. Arms, torso, head and support can share one spatialPose. Avoid independently moving a parent under a held hand without including its arm track.

Use prepare for a preparatory gesture before audio is ready, speech for the main expressive trajectory. A single speech stage can contain a multi-key trajectory; do not wait for a whole spoken sentence between every hand movement. No word-level synchronization is promised.

Read Current Pose as authoritative. Ordinary conversation can leave a hold untouched; no repeated angle output is required. Only reset or replace when appropriate to the user's intent. Do not claim an impossible movement succeeded. Small geometric corrections are allowed; rejected movements produce diagnostics without an automatic model retry.
